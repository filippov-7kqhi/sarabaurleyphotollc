Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 8, 2026

Sara Baurley Photo LLC (“we,” “us,” “our”), a Florida limited liability company, operates sarabaurleyphotollc.shop (the “Site”). This policy explains what information we collect through the Site, how we use it, and the choices you have.

Information we collect

  • Information you give us. When you send an inquiry through the contact form, we receive your name, email address, phone number (if provided), session type, preferred date, location and message.
  • Technical information. Like most websites, our hosting provider may automatically log your IP address, browser type, device information and the pages you request, for security and reliability.

How we use information

We use your information to respond to your inquiry, prepare quotes, schedule and deliver photography sessions, keep records of our business, and protect the Site from abuse. We do not sell your personal information.

Cookies and analytics

At the time of writing, the Site does not use advertising or analytics trackers. If we add tools such as Google Analytics or the Meta Pixel in the future, they may set cookies or collect information such as your IP address, device details and pages viewed, and we will update this policy to say so. You can control cookies through your browser settings.

Who we share information with

We share information only with service providers that help us run the Site and handle form submissions (for example, our website host and form-processing provider), and only so they can perform those services. We may also disclose information when required by law or to protect our rights.

Client photographs

Photographs taken during a session are handled under the written agreement for that session, including any permission to display them publicly.

How long we keep information

We keep inquiry information for as long as needed to respond to you and maintain our business records, and then delete or anonymize it.

Your choices

You may ask us to access, correct or delete the personal information you have given us by contacting us through the contact form.

Children

The Site is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date above shows when it was last revised.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Reach us through the contact form.
